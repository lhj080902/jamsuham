## 4. Reporting and blocking
- **Block**: tap [Block] next to a nickname in the Players tab, or long-press a chat line and choose "Block the sender". That person's chat is hidden on this device. You can undo it in Settings → Blocked users.
- **Hide a message**: long-press a chat line and choose "Hide this message" to remove that line from the screen.
- **Report**: tap [Report] in the Players tab, or long-press a chat line and choose "Report this message". An email draft opens with the report filled in. When you send it, it goes to the developer.
- Blocking and hiding apply only to the screen of this device. The other person stays on that server.
